From 40 reviews to 210 in six weeks.

A debut novel with strong early word of mouth had stalled at forty reviews and no clear audience beyond the author's own circle. We placed it with readers already discussing quiet, character-driven fiction. Within six weeks it had two hundred and ten reviews, none of them solicited with payment.
